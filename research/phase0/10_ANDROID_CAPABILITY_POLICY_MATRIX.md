# Android / Google Play Capability Audit

**Snapshot date:** 2026-08-25  
**Purpose:** technical architecture and policy-risk triage, not legal advice. Google Play approval is an external decision and must be re-checked before release.

## Classification rules

- `PLAY_SAFE_OR_LIKELY`: ordinary platform use appears compatible when it is necessary, disclosed, least-privilege, and user-controlled.
- `PLAY_RESTRICTED_REQUIRES_REVIEW`: a special access, sensitive/high-risk permission, role, declaration, foreground-service review, or unsettled agentic use makes approval/use-case review necessary.
- `ADVANCED_SIDELOAD_ONLY`: Kinetic's contemplated use is incompatible with the stated Play design constraint or current Play policy even if Android technically exposes the API.
- `SYSTEM_PRIVILEGED_ONLY`: the useful form requires an OEM/platform signature, device-owner provisioning, or comparable privileged environment.
- `UNKNOWN_REQUIRES_POLICY_RESEARCH`: the surface is preview/unsettled or its acceptability depends on a runtime not yet defined.

The classification applies to **Kinetic's proposed use**, not to every app that uses the API. Runtime permissions are necessary but never sufficient: purpose limitation, prominent disclosure/consent, Data safety declarations, retention, and deletion still apply.

## Local evidence that capability separation is practical

OpenClaw already implements the most relevant packaging boundary:

- `Eco_reference/openclaw/apps/android/app/build.gradle.kts` defines `play` and `thirdParty` product flavors in one Android module.
- `Eco_reference/openclaw/apps/android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml` contains the shared, narrower manifest surface.
- `Eco_reference/openclaw/apps/android/app/src/thirdParty/AndroidManifest.xml` adds background location, SMS, call-log, broader media access, and an AccessibilityService only outside the Play source set.
- `Eco_reference/openclaw/apps/android/app/src/thirdParty/java/ai/openclaw/app/accessibility/OpenClawAccessibilityService.kt` and its action/snapshot peers are therefore structurally separable from the shared client.

The counterexamples show why Kinetic cannot adopt one broad manifest unchanged:

- `Eco_reference/opendroid/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml` combines AccessibilityService, notification access, overlays, exact alarms, `QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES`, `MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, SMS, background location, and a `specialUse` foreground service in one build.
- `Eco_reference/AndyClaw/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml` additionally declares signature/privileged operations such as `WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS`, `INSTALL_PACKAGES`, `FORCE_STOP_PACKAGES`, `REBOOT`, and privileged Bluetooth/telephony controls; these reflect its ethOS/system-app environment, not an ordinary Play app.

**Kinetic implication:** keep one kernel and one capability descriptor model, but bind capability implementations through build-time allowlists and manifests. An advanced artifact must never be discoverable or activatable from the Play artifact.

## AppFunctions research slot

Android AppFunctions should be a first-class **capability provider**, not the Kinetic kernel's only tool representation:

```text
Kinetic Tool (stable, provider-neutral contract)
        <->
Kinetic Capability Descriptor (scope, risk, schemas, availability)
        <->
Android AppFunction adapter (discovery, invocation, result/error mapping)
```

As of this snapshot, official Android documentation calls AppFunctions an **experimental preview**, available on Android 16+, with the Jetpack integration compiling against API 36+; callers need `EXECUTE_APP_FUNCTIONS`, and end-to-end agent access is not generally available to all apps/system agents. This makes the bridge `UNKNOWN_REQUIRES_POLICY_RESEARCH` for architecture-freeze purposes, while still making it the most strategically important Android-native integration spike.

Required conceptual changes to reference designs:

- **AndyClaw-style IPC:** replace extension-specific AIDL method families and unprotected discovery with one internal adapter that converts discovered AppFunction metadata into Kinetic descriptors. Preserve caller identity, package/signing identity, cancellation, typed errors, and provider-side confirmations. Do not require every app to implement Kinetic-specific AIDL.
- **OpenClaw-style tools:** separate the JSON tool contract from Node execution. Register an `AppFunctionToolProvider` whose availability is device/API/package dependent; pass all calls through Kinetic policy and confirmation before platform invocation.
- **Kinetic descriptor:** retain metadata Android AppFunctions may not express (Kinetic scopes, risk, data sensitivity, network/foreground/audit policy) as a local overlay keyed by provider identity + function identifier.
- **Compatibility:** offer explicit intent/deep-link or app-specific Binder/provider adapters below Android 16; never emulate missing AppFunctions with Accessibility in the Play artifact.

## Decision

The Play artifact should be useful without Accessibility, broad package/storage visibility, package installation, root/Shizuku, exact alarms, persistent unrestricted foreground service, or remote executable extensions. Advanced and system/OEM adapters can implement the same internal capability interface, but capability inclusion, manifest permissions, discovery, and signing must be build-time separated—not a runtime flag hidden inside the Play binary.
